Your card’s name, wherever you need it.
Replaced with the card name when rendered.

[-MECHANIC]Reminder only

Only the description, in italic parentheses. No name or cost.

[MECHANIC {2}]Name + cost + reminder

The mechanic name, cost, and description. Your cost replaces {ref} in the description; numbers or mana symbols work.

[MECHANIC]Name + reminder

The mechanic name and description, with {ref} removed.

Replace MECHANIC with a mechanic from your collection.
